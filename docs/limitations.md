# Limitations

- Hats and beanies may be confused with helmets.
- Small heads and distant riders may be missed.
- Rear views, occlusion, and unusual lighting or angles affect classification.
- Crowded scenes and multiple riders make person-to-motorcycle association difficult.
- A missed helmet prediction does not prove that a rider is unhelmeted.
- Small, angled, partially occluded, or blurred plates may be missed.
- OCR can return unreadable or incorrect text; Cambodian plate layouts may be especially challenging.
- There is no verified end-to-end video or live-camera evaluation in this repository.

This application is an assistive prototype. Its output is not a guaranteed enforcement decision.
